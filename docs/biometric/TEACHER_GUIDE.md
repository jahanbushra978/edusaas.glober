# Teacher Guide - Biometric Attendance

## Overview
Teachers can view their own attendance records through the teacher portal.

## Viewing Attendance
1. Login to your teacher account
2. Navigate to your profile/dashboard
3. View attendance history with dates, punch in/out times, and status

## Biometric Profile
- Your biometric profile links your ZKTeco device PIN to your system account
- Contact admin to update biometric settings

## Status Descriptions
- **Present**: On-time arrival (before 9:30 AM)
- **Late**: Arrived after 9:30 AM
- **Absent**: No punch recorded for the day
- **Half Day**: Less than 4 working hours
